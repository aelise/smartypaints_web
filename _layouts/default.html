Find a Dupe

Don't have that paint? Find what you do have.

See how any paint stacks up against what you already own. Not just by color, by behavior. Because a "close enough" that stains when you want lifting? Not close enough.

Match to Tutorials

Inside info on any tutorial.

Paste a link, see which of your paints match the instructor's. Where they don't, Smarty Paints tells you why and suggests swaps. Takes 30 seconds and beats winging it.
